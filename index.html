Overview

About Bus Pass Management System

The Bus Pass Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify the process of applying for and managing student bus passes. Students can register, apply online and track their application status, while administrators can manage and process applications digitally.

  • 100% paperless student registration and approval cycle
  • Instant demo fee payment confirmation & printable receipts
  • Comprehensive administrator control panel for approvals
